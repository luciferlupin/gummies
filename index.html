FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about HER BITE gummies

Are your gummies really all-natural?

+

Yes! All HER BITE gummies are made with 100% natural ingredients. We use real fruit purees, natural flavors, and colors derived from fruits and vegetables. No artificial preservatives, colors, or flavors ever.

What is your shipping policy?

+

We offer free standard shipping on orders $25+. Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping (2-3 days) is available for $9.99. All orders are processed within 24 hours on business days.

What is your return policy?

+

We stand behind our products with a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not completely satisfied, return your order within 30 days for a full refund. Return shipping is free for defective products.

Are your gummies suitable for children?

+

Yes! Our gummies are perfect for children ages 2 and up. They're made with natural ingredients, contain no artificial additives, and are fortified with vitamin C. However, they should be consumed in moderation as part of a balanced diet.

How long do the gummies stay fresh?

+

Our gummies have a shelf life of 12 months from the production date. For best taste and texture, we recommend consuming within 6 months of opening. Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

Do you offer bulk ordering for events?

+

Yes! We offer bulk pricing for orders of 10+ bags. Perfect for parties, corporate events, or special occasions. Contact our sales team at sales@herbite.com for custom quotes and packaging options.
